4 Siblings Motorcycle Service

An operations system design for a motorcycle repair shop, covering job orders, parts usage, and customer records.

Business analysisReal clientSolution design

Solution design for a client. No public site or code.

The problem

4 Siblings Motorcycle Service wanted better visibility into its daily operations. We started by looking at how the shop handles job tracking, job orders, customer records, and parts usage, and where those processes break down.

What I did

  • Interviewed the client to map current workflows and diagnose pain points in job tracking, job orders, customer records, and parts usage.
  • Analyzed business processes and translated findings into data requirements, KPIs, and system features.
  • Led the proposed solution design, including BI dashboards for operational visibility, and validated it with the client.
  • Authored the project charter defining scope, objectives, stakeholders, budget, and deliverables.

What the system covers

  • Job order tracking

    Follow each job from intake to release.

  • Parts usage

    See which parts are used and how often.

  • Customer records

    Service history in one place.

  • BI dashboards

    KPIs that give the owner operational visibility.
