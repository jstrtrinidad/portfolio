4 Siblings Motorcycle Service

A searchable operations system for a motorcycle repair shop that ran on paper, with dashboards for the owner.

Project ManagerDeveloperTeam of 5Real client

In client testing. The code is private.

The problem

4 Siblings Motorcycle Service ran on paper. Finding a job order or a customer's service history meant digging through records, and each lookup took 10 minutes or more. The owner also had no clear view of how the shop was performing.

What I did

  • Led a 5-member team through on-site discovery at the shop, mapping the paper-based workflows behind each slow record lookup.
  • Built a searchable Laravel system for job orders and live repair status, cutting record lookups to under 2 minutes in client testing.
  • Built 3 Power BI dashboards tracking 7 KPIs, including turnaround time, revenue by service type, and repeat customer rate, giving the owner a clear view of shop performance for the first time.

What it does

  • Searchable job orders

    Find any job order in under 2 minutes instead of digging through paper.

  • Live repair status

    See where each motorcycle is in the repair process.

  • Owner dashboards

    3 Power BI dashboards built on the shop's own data.

  • 7 KPIs

    Turnaround time, revenue by service type, repeat customer rate, and more.

How it works

The system runs on Laravel with a MySQL database. The same shop data feeds the Power BI dashboards, so the owner sees turnaround time, revenue by service type, and repeat customers without any extra paperwork.
