Komyut AI

A web app that helps Metro Manila commuters plan better trips, with AI suggestions on when to leave and how crowded the ride will be.

UI/UX and front endTeam of 4React and OpenAI
GitHub repo

The problem

Commuters in Metro Manila need more than a route. They need to know which mode of transport to take, when to leave, and how packed the ride will be.

Komyut AI (proposed as CommuteSmart AI) takes an origin, destination, and travel time, then suggests routes, the best time to travel, and alternatives when a route is too crowded.

What I did

  • Designed the UI/UX and wireframes for the app.
  • Developed front-end screens in React and Tailwind CSS.
  • Built the Google Maps API integration and user authentication with a teammate.
  • Helped define the vision, scope, features, and user flow at kickoff, studying apps like Google Maps, Citymapper, Sakay.ph, and Moovit as references.

What it does

  • Smart route planner

    Plan a trip between two locations with different routes and transport options.

  • Best time to travel

    AI suggestions on when to leave to avoid rush hour.

  • Crowd level predictor

    Estimates how crowded a route or ride will be.

  • Saved trips

    Save, edit, and delete regular commutes.

  • Accounts

    Sign in for a personalized experience.

  • Commute dashboard

    Most traveled routes, busiest hours, and average commute time.

How it works

The front end is React with Tailwind CSS. Routes come from the Google Maps API, the travel-time and crowd suggestions come from the OpenAI API, and saved trips are stored in MongoDB.
