How it started

In Grade 10, I told my mom I wanted to study at a UAAP school in Manila. She laughed it off, and I took that as a challenge.

My first try, reselling clothes on Facebook Marketplace, got zero sales. Then I noticed how much work it took students to set up Notion by hand, so I made a ready-to-use Notion dashboard. It sold within days. From there, customer feedback led me to digital planners and reading planners.

The business helped pay for my studies and got me to UST.

What I did

  • Built and ran the business on my own, growing it to ₱1M+ in revenue within 18 months.
  • Found that customers struggled with the manual setup of digital systems, so I launched ready-to-use templates that sold 2,000+ copies.
  • Reached 4,000+ total sales with a 4.8-star rating from 587 reviews.
  • Used sales data and customer feedback to decide which products, prices, and designs to improve and build next.

What the business does
