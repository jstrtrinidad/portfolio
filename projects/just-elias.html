Forward Planner

My digital business. I sell digital planners on Etsy through Forward Planner and run paid promotions for app and digital product brands.

FounderSince 2023₱1M+ revenue
Visit the Etsy shop

The problem

I started Forward Planner in January 2023 with two revenue streams: selling my own digital products and services, and promoting third-party apps and digital products for partner brands.

Growing both meant deciding, again and again, where to spend limited time: which platform, which format, and what to charge each partner.

What I did

  • Founded and scaled the business to ₱1M+ in revenue within 18 months.
  • Analyzed engagement data across 3 platforms and shifted strategy to short-form video, the top-performing format, growing a combined organic audience of 500K+.
  • Managed 30+ local and international partnerships with app and digital product brands.
  • Used audience data and past campaign performance to negotiate pricing and deliverables.
  • Secured 2 year-long contracts and 10+ repeat clients.

What the business does

  • Digital products

    Planners and digital products sold through the Forward Planner shop on Etsy.

  • Brand promotions

    Paid campaigns for app and digital product brands, local and international.

  • Data-led content

    Format decisions based on engagement data across platforms.

  • Repeat partnerships

    Long-term deals built on past campaign results.

How it works

Short-form video performed best across the 3 platforms, so the content strategy moved there. The same audience and campaign data became the basis for pricing each partnership.
